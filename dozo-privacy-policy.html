Privacy Policy

Dozo Samurai Legends

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy describes how the game Dozo Samurai Legends handles information. The game keeps progress on your device. It does not run its own account server, and this version does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Information stored on your device

Game progress is saved in local storage on the device under the key dozo.meta.v1. That save can include:

  • Currencies and items, such as gold, gems, stamina, summon tickets, and upgrade materials
  • Owned cards, party, stage progress, stars, and shop purchases already granted
  • Settings, including whether music and sound effects are on

This save stays on the device. The game does not upload it.

Sign in with Apple

On iOS you can choose Sign in with Apple. The sign-in request asks Apple for your name and email. The game does not save the name or the email. It stores only the Apple user identifier, in the iOS Keychain on that device (com.dozo.samurai.auth). The identifier is not sent to a game server.

Apple processes the sign-in under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Sign in with Apple is not offered in the editor or web preview.

Purchases

On iOS, purchases go through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles payment. The game does not receive your card number or billing address. It receives the product identifier and the transaction result so it can grant the item on the device. Product identifiers start with com.dozo.samurai..

In the editor and on the web preview, the shop grants items directly and does not contact the App Store.

What this version does not do

  • No account server, and no sale of personal information
  • No third-party analytics SDK
  • No advertising. The AdSense option in the project is off
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or microphone

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you delete the app or clear its data. There is no online account to delete. Removing the app removes the local save. The Apple user identifier is kept only in the device Keychain.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the game after the update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

This build does not include a support email. Use the contact method shown on the App Store product page.
